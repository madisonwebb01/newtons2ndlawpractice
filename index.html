Newton's 2nd Law

Fnet = maa = Fnet ÷ m  ·  m = Fnet ÷ a

Kinematics

vf = vi + at
Δx = vit + ½at2
vf2 = vi2 + 2aΔx

Units

Solving steps

  1. List the givens and the unknown, with units.
  2. Find Fnet. Draw an FBD: same direction adds, opposite directions subtract.
  3. a = Fnet ÷ m. The acceleration points the same way as Fnet.
  4. Need a velocity, distance or time? Use a in a kinematics equation.
